# RSI Perseus: Production Announcement

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**  
*Roberts Space Industries - New Austin, Terra*

After years of refinement and testing, Roberts Space Industries proudly announces that the **RSI Perseus** has officially transitioned from concept development into full production. This marks a significant milestone for the sub-capital gunship program.

![Perseus Concept Art](https://placeholder-image-url/perseus-concept.jpg)

## Production Specifications

### Updated Design Elements

The production Perseus incorporates numerous improvements based on extensive testing:

#### Hull & Armor
- **Reinforced Plating**: 40mm composite armor (up from 35mm concept)
- **Modular Sections**: 8 replaceable armor panels for field maintenance
- **Emergency Bulkheads**: Enhanced compartmentalization for combat survivability

#### Weapon Systems

**Primary Armament:**
| Weapon Type | Mount Size | Quantity | Fire Arc |
|-------------|------------|----------|----------|
| Laser Cannons | S5 | 4x | Forward |
| Torpedo Tubes | S9 | 4x | Forward |
| Point Defense | S2 | 8x | 360° |

**Secondary Systems:**
- Advanced targeting suite with quantum radar integration  
- EMP hardening for all critical systems
- Ammunition fabrication capability for extended operations

## Key Features

### Multi-Crew Operations
- **Bridge Crew**: 4-6 personnel optimal
- **Engineering**: Dedicated damage control station
- **Gunnery**: Independent turret control systems

### Mission Profiles
The Perseus excels in:

1. **Escort Operations** - Protecting larger vessels from fighter threats
2. **Patrol Missions** - System security and anti-piracy operations  
3. **Strike Missions** - Precision attacks on capital-class targets

## Production Timeline

> **Phase 1**: Hull fabrication begins Q1 2945
> 
> **Phase 2**: Systems integration and testing Q2-Q3 2945
> 
> **Phase 3**: Initial delivery to UEE Navy Q4 2945

### Civilian Availability

Limited civilian sales will begin **18 months** after military deliveries commence, with the following restrictions:

- Maximum of 2 units per individual/organization
- Weapon systems subject to local jurisdiction laws
- Mandatory registration with UEE Ship Registry
