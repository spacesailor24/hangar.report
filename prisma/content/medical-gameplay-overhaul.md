# Medical Gameplay Revolution

Cloud Imperium Games has unveiled the most comprehensive medical system overhaul in Star Citizen's development history. This massive update introduces revolutionary gameplay mechanics that will fundamentally change how players interact with injury, healing, and medical emergencies throughout the verse.

## Key Features

### Tiered Medical Facilities
The new system introduces **three distinct tiers** of medical facilities:

- **Tier 1**: Basic first aid stations found on smaller outposts
- **Tier 2**: Advanced medical bays on larger stations and capital ships  
- **Tier 3**: Full hospital complexes on major planets and medical stations

### Advanced Injury System
Players will now experience:

> **Complex Injury States**: Bleeding, fractures, infections, and organ damage
> 
> **Recovery Times**: Realistic healing periods requiring actual rest
> 
> **Permanent Consequences**: Some injuries may leave lasting effects

### Medical Equipment Expansion

The update introduces over **20 new medical devices**:

| Device | Function | Tier Required |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| Med-Gun Mk2 | Advanced wound treatment | Tier 2+ |
| Surgical Suite | Complex operations | Tier 3 |
| Bacta Tank | Accelerated healing | Tier 3 |

## Professional Opportunities

Medical professionals will gain access to:

1. **Specialized Careers**: Become a field medic, surgeon, or emergency responder
2. **Medical Licenses**: Earn certifications for advanced procedures
3. **Emergency Contracts**: Respond to crisis situations across the verse

*"This isn't just about healing - it's about creating meaningful careers in medicine,"* says Lead Designer Sarah Chen.
